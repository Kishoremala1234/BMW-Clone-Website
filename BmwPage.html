About BMW M4

The BMW M4 is a high-performance luxury sports coupe engineered for drivers who demand precision, speed, and premium comfort. Powered by a 3.0L BMW M TwinPower Turbo inline-6 engine, the M4 delivers breathtaking acceleration, razor-sharp handling, and track-inspired dynamics.

Its aggressive exterior design, aerodynamic body lines, and signature kidney grille create a commanding road presence. Inside the cabin, premium leather upholstery, curved digital displays, ambient lighting, and intelligent driver assistance systems provide a refined yet thrilling driving experience.
